# 测试员欢迎邮件模板（有人回复邮箱后发送）

> 用途：测试员在 Reddit 私信/回帖给出邮箱后，你把 TA 加进 Play Console 邮箱列表，
> 再发这封欢迎邮件（含 opt-in 链接和注意事项）。也可以直接用 Play Console 自带的
> "给测试员发邮件"功能批量发送。

---

**Subject: You're in! HappyEat closed testing — opt-in link inside 🎮**

Hi!

Thanks for volunteering to test **HappyEat**, my little arcade game. You're on the tester list — here's how to get started:

**1. Join the test (30 seconds)**
Open this link with the Google account you gave me and press "Become a tester":
👉 【贴上你的 Opt-in 链接】

**2. Install**
The Play Store page will appear — install like any app.

**3. What Google needs (important!)**
Please **keep the app installed for 14 days** and open it now and then. Google counts this to let my game publish — dropping it early hurts the countdown.

**4. Play & report (fun part)**
Slide in the bottom move area to catch food and press JUMP to dodge beasts. Use two fingers together; swap the controls in Settings. Grab a car for temporary protection.
Send me a screenshot of your best score from the in-game leaderboard — and any feedback is gold.

Thanks a million! 🙏

## 批量小技巧

- Play Console 邮箱列表直接支持粘贴逗号/换行分隔的邮箱（每张列表上限 2000 个），
  不需要真的建邮件组
- Console → 测试 → 封闭测试 → 测试人员 → 「发送反馈」可以给全列表群发更新通知，
  每隔 3-4 天发一次当提醒
